# Facebook follows

The Snanify Page (facebook.com/snanify) follows Pages and joins groups here, at most five a
day, by the scheduled task `snanify-facebook-follows`. The task reads this file, acts, and writes
back what it did. Edit any section by hand: the task respects it.

## Rules the task works under

- At most 5 actions a day, Pages and groups together. Stop at the first Facebook warning.
- Act only as the Snanify Page, never as Sven.
- Follow and join only. Never post, comment, like, message or invite.
- A group that asks membership questions or wants its rules accepted goes to "Needs Sven",
  never joined by the task.

## What counts as relevant

- Hindu devotion tied to the six waters: Ganga, Yamuna, Godavari, Shipra, Kaveri, the Sangam,
  Haridwar, Prayagraj, Mathura, Nashik, Ujjain.
- The calendar: Kartik snan, Ekadashi, Purnima, panchang, Kumbh, Chhath, Diwali.
- Hindu temples and community associations abroad: US, UK, Canada, the Gulf, Australia,
  Singapore.
- Indian diaspora community groups in those countries, English or Hindi.
- Active (a post in the last month), and over 1,000 members or followers.

Never: political or communal groups, anything hostile to another faith, astrology or remedy
sellers, matchmaking, buy and sell, jobs, visa or immigration agents, crypto, spam.
